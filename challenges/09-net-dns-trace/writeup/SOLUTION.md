# DNS Record Hunt — Instructor Solution

**Correct flag:** `CTF{192.0.2.44}`

## How to solve

The A record maps the hostname to IPv4 address 192.0.2.44.

## Learning objective

Identify a DNS A record and distinguish it from other record types.

## Suggested tools

Text editor

## Teaching note

Have learners describe the evidence they used, not just submit the flag. The attached artifact is fictional and designed for an isolated classroom exercise.
