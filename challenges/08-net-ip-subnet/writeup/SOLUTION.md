# Same Subnet? — Instructor Solution

**Correct flag:** `CTF{192.168.12.127}`

## How to solve

A /26 has 64 total addresses per subnet. The range starting at .64 ends at .127, the broadcast address.

## Learning objective

Calculate IPv4 /26 network boundaries.

## Suggested tools

Calculator or scratch paper

## Teaching note

Have learners describe the evidence they used, not just submit the flag. The attached artifact is fictional and designed for an isolated classroom exercise.
