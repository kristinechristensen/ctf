# Permissions Puzzle — Instructor Solution

**Correct flag:** `CTF{754}`

## How to solve

For -rwxr-xr--, the owner permissions are 7, group permissions are 5, and other permissions are 4. Thus 754.

## Learning objective

Read Unix mode bits and convert them to octal notation.

## Suggested tools

Text editor

## Teaching note

Have learners describe the evidence they used, not just submit the flag. The attached artifact is fictional and designed for an isolated classroom exercise.
