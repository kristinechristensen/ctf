# Disk Space Detective — Instructor Solution

**Correct flag:** `CTF{/var}`

## How to solve

Locate the Use% column. The /var filesystem is at 98%, exceeding the 95% threshold.

## Learning objective

Interpret disk usage and recognize dangerously full volumes.

## Suggested tools

Text editor

## Teaching note

Have learners describe the evidence they used, not just submit the flag. The attached artifact is fictional and designed for an isolated classroom exercise.
